# Character Dossiers

### 1. Lyra Voss — Chief Chronochartographer
- **Affiliation:** Jovian Proof-of-History Observatory, Ganymede Station.
- **Specialization:** Relativistic VDF telemetry and sequential hash chain topology.
- **Background:** Born in the subterranean habitats of Callisto, Lyra has spent two decades measuring the precise gravitational lensing effects of Jupiter on electromagnetic signal propagation. Her intuition for cryptographic anomalies is legendary across the Outer Colonies.

### 2. Commander Kaelen Thrace — Entropy Arbiter
- **Affiliation:** United Outer Fleet Security Division.
- **Specialization:** Orbital ballistics, propulsion grid defense, and consensus governance.
- **Background:** A former deep-space patrol officer who survived the Phobos Fuel Mutiny, Thrace views cryptographic integrity not as abstract mathematics, but as the thin pressure boundary separating 4 million colonists from hard vacuum.

### 3. VDF-8070 — Autonomous State Sentinel
- **Classification:** Class-IV Photonic Cryptographic Co-Processor.
- **Role:** High-throughput slot verification, transaction ordering validation, and zero-knowledge proof generation.
- **Quirk:** Refuses to process any heuristic that cannot be expressed as an immutable quadratic arithmetic program.
