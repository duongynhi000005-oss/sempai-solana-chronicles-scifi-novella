# World Lore & Hard Sci-Fi Physics Foundations

### 1. The Physics of Verifiable Delay Functions (VDF)
In our universe, light takes between 33 and 53 minutes to travel between Earth and Jupiter depending on orbital alignment. Traditional consensus algorithms (such as Paxos, Raft, or classic Byzantine Fault Tolerance) fail catastrophically at astronomical distances because message synchronization requires multi-round-trip confirmations.

The **Chrono-Anchor Grid** solves this through sequential cryptographic hash chains computed on specialized quantum-resistant photonic crystals. Each tick $T_{i+1} = \mathcal{H}(T_i)$ requires precisely $0.4$ nanoseconds of physical state transition and cannot be parallelized, even with Dyson-scale computational swarms. This proves that real physical time has elapsed between event logs without requiring inter-station communication.

### 2. The Jovian Schedulers
Ganymede Station houses the largest validator core in human history: the **Agave-Jovian Titan**, an array of helium-3 cooled superconductor chips anchored into Ganymede's icy bedrock. It continuously ingests trade telemetry, atmospheric dome pressure logs, and life-support energy vouchers from Callisto, Europa, and Io.

### 3. Relativistic Slashing
If a colony station attempts to double-spend hydrogen reserves by broadcasting conflicting commitments along divergent light cones, the Chrono-Anchor Grid detects the signature equivocation at the first intersection point. The penalty is absolute: cryptographic slashing of station orbital maneuvering fuel tokens, leaving the offending station in an immutable ballistic trajectory until audited.
