# Chapter 2: Relativistic Slashing

Commander Kaelen Thrace entered the telemetry deck with heavy, magnetic boots that clanged rhythmically against the ribbed alloy flooring. His dark thermal suit bore the insignia of the Jovian Fleet Arbiter—a pair of calipers crossed over an elliptical orbit.

"Tell me this isn't another ghost in the photonic core, Lyra," Thrace said, his sharp gray eyes scanning the frantic visualizations flashing across the curved holoscreens. "Callisto Station is already rationing atmospheric heating. If the fuel shipments don't clear the settlement ledger by midnight, half a million people will be freezing in their bunks."

"It's not a ghost, Kaelen," Lyra replied without looking up from her calculations. "It's a hostile relativistic wedge. Look at this telemetry from Lagrange point Four."

She projected a three-dimensional topographic map of the Jovian gravity well. At the outer rim of the radiation belt, a heavy mining dreadnought—the *Aethelgard*, registered to an unregistered offshore mining syndicate—was firing its plasma thrusters in a tight retrograde burn.

"The *Aethelgard* isn't just mining ice," Lyra explained, pointing to a concentrated distortion in the gravitational gradient. "They've slung a microscopic primordial black hole—massing less than ten million metric tons, captured from the Oort belt—into a parabolic pass directly between our Chrono-Pulsar and Ganymede's receiver."

Thrace leaned forward, his weathered jaw tightening. "Gravitational time dilation."

"Exactly," Lyra said. "The gravitational potential of the singularity slows down the propagation of our VDF hash stream relative to their private local validator. They are creating a localized bubble where their own transactions appear to have been signed *before* ours, even though they were generated thirty seconds later. When the consensus layer compares the two streams, our legitimate life-support vouchers will be slashed as double-spends."

VDF-8070’s status indicator pulsed amber. "Equivocation penalty is programmed into the consensus root: any node broadcasting an inconsistent sequence state suffers automatic forfeiture of all fuel routing credits. If the *Aethelgard*'s forged block is accepted by the network, Ganymede and Callisto will be permanently deregistered from the orbital power grid."

"We need to burn their fork out of the sky," Thrace said coldly, tapping his tactical gauntlet.

"We can't fire at them," Lyra retorted. "The micro-singularity would destabilize and sling directly into Callisto's orbital plane. We have to defeat them mathematically. We have to prove to the entire Sol network that their timestamp was warped by artificial curvature before the network reaches finality."
