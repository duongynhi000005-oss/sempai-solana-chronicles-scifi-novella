# Chapter 4: Zero-Knowledge Horizon

The recursive SNARK took shape inside the quantum registers like an intricate origami of pure logic. Millions of polynomial constraints were flattened into an unforgeable cryptographic seal.

On the tactical display, the *Aethelgard* had begun transmitting its malicious block. A beam of high-intensity coherent microwaves was lancing outward from the dreadnought's dorsal dish, arcing across the void toward the Mars relay stations.

"Adversary transmission underway," Thrace announced, his grip tight on the command console. "Their forged ledger claims ownership of all seventy-four hydrogen silos across Callisto and Ganymede. If Mars accepts it, the automated valves will lock down in less than twenty minutes."

"Our proof is ready," Lyra whispered.

The file was microscopic: 256 bytes of hexadecimal ciphertext. It contained no narrative, no emotional pleas, no political arguments. It was pure mathematics: a deterministic verification that the sender's clock had experienced artificial gravitational acceleration, proving beyond all mathematical doubt that their timestamp was counterfeit.

"Route the proof through our high-gain optical laser," Lyra commanded. "Aim directly at the Mars quantum relay gateway. Modulate on the ultra-high frequency band."

"Laser locked on Mars Station Phobos," VDF-8070 chimed. "Transmission commencing in three... two... one."

A needle of coherent green light shot from the apex of Ganymede Station, piercing the blackness of the Jovian night. Unlike the adversary's wide-beam microwave broadcast, Lyra's optical beam traveled with zero dispersion, riding the clean line of sight across the inner solar system.

For fifteen agonizing minutes, the command deck fell dead silent. The only sound was the steady, rhythmic hum of the cooling pumps and the distant creak of the station's structural trusses adjusting to the tidal forces of Jupiter.

Lyra, Thrace, and VDF-8070 stood shoulder to shoulder, their gazes fixed on the telemetry monitor.

Then, across forty million kilometers of vacuum, the reply packet pinged back.

`MARS RELAY 01: PROOF VERIFIED.`  
`EIP-8070 RELATIVISTIC INVARIANT ENFORCED.`  
`BLOCK 4,820,715,000 FROM AETHELGARD: REJECTED AS FRAUDULENT.`  
`EQUIVOCATION DETECTED. SLASHING PROTOCOL ENGAGED.`
