# Chapter 5: Immutable Consensus

The tactical holoscreen flared with sudden brilliance.

Out near Jupiter's L4 Lagrange point, the automated consensus protocol had triggered the network-wide slashing contract. Every smart contract governing the *Aethelgard*'s fuel depots, its orbital docking rights, and its automated supply drones executed simultaneously and irreversibly.

The dreadnought's main thrusters cut out as its digital authorization keys were revoked across all interplanetary registries. Deprived of orbital compensation commands, the ship drifted helplessly along its ballistic trajectory, safely missing the Jovian shipping lanes.

Back in Ganymede's observation bay, the emerald stream of VDF ticks resumed its clean, uninterrupted cascade.

"Tick four billion, eight hundred and twenty million, seven hundred and sixteen," Lyra read aloud. Her hands were steady now, the adrenaline slowly giving way to a profound sense of relief. "Entropy delta: normal. Sequential order restored. Callisto fuel vouchers: confirmed and finalized."

Commander Thrace let out a long breath and rested his hand on Lyra's shoulder. "Callisto just confirmed receipt of the life-support allocations. The heating grids in the residential sectors are firing up right now. You saved half a million souls tonight, Chronochartographer."

Lyra smiled faintly, looking out through the transparent viewport. Jupiter hung before them, colossal and eternal, its bands of red and gold swirling in majestic indifference to the fleeting struggles of humanity.

Beside her, Sentinel VDF-8070 dimmed its optical sensors to a calm, steady azure. "Consensus is not merely the agreement of machines," the synthetic voice noted softly. "It is the commitment that reality cannot be rewritten by force."

Lyra nodded, watching the thin green beam of the Chrono-Pulsar pulse across the void, knitting the scattered outposts of humanity together with threads of immutable time. As long as the ticks held true, the frontier would endure.
